First alpha of Powder Toy Next, a GPU falling-sand sandbox for Android.

## Install

Download `powder-toy-next-v0.1.0-alpha.1.apk` below on your phone, open it, and allow installs from your browser when Android asks.

## What's in it

- A 2D sandbox at your phone's native resolution, one cell per screen pixel, simulated on the GPU
- 10 materials: wall, sand, water, ice, steam, stone, lava, wood, oil and fire
- Heat that conducts between cells, phase changes (ice melts, water boils, stone melts into lava), and burning
- A 4:3 sim area at full screen width (1080 x 1440 on a Pixel 10 Pro XL) over a keyboard-height control panel: material keys, tools (paint, erase, heat, cool), brush size, pause, step and speed
- The sim area stays clear of the status bar and camera cutout
- An on-device benchmark under Menu

## Known limits

- Android only, 64-bit (arm64) phones with Vulkan
- First alpha, tested on one phone so far
- Signed with a throwaway test key, so a later build will not install over this one; uninstall it first
- Sand falls slowly over a full screen height, and hot air does not rise yet
